# A2T Executable Ground Build V1 Verdict

## Decision

The static build passes. Permit only a separately preregistered local-tokenizer prompt-fit check.
Do not open a model flight, ground qualification, candidate pruning, training-free controller,
approximately-1,000 scale, baseline change, test access, or Stage-3 claim yet.

## Result

The deterministic handbook contains all 152 registry tools, including the one registry-only tool,
and summarizes all 354 argument-pattern families and all 85 argument keys observed across 151
construction tools. Twenty-eight `(domain, category)` contrast groups organize the complete tool
space without reducing it. The handbook is 54,881 ASCII UTF-8 bytes and 1,344 lines, below the
frozen 65,536-byte build cap. Its SHA-256 is
`58ba08f3cebbe390156506653051d54aa37796822f49f5b851fd6eba4818fbc8`.

The first and reversed-pool builds are byte-identical. The handbook renders zero construction
utterances, zero argument values, and zero complete registry-default literals. It is built from
12,601 construction units / 22,951 samples, with zero intersection against the exposed 400 or the
proposed unexposed 400-item evaluation tier.

This is a content and safety result only. Byte fit is not token fit, and full coverage is not model
comprehension. The owner-set performance contract remains untested: exact at least 0.90, tool at
least 0.95, slot micro F1 at least 0.90, parse rate at least 0.99, with exact 0.95 as the target.

## Next gate

The tokenizer check must render the exact proposed system prompt, the frozen completion allowance,
and the measured worst speech-feature envelope under the same 32,768-token per-slot context used by
CPV2. It must use the pinned local tokenizer, record its identity and contact count, and fail closed
unless the full handbook fits with positive margin. It may not shorten, reorder, or select handbook
entries after seeing token counts; an overflow closes V1 and requires a separately preregistered
model-free compression successor.

## Integrity and engineering scope

The build recorded zero outbound attempts and zero model, retriever, embedding, tokenizer, network,
audio, or GPU contact. External receipt SHA-256:
`505645b3cdef553ac847a5b248936182160d7b38aaaac84a076b9137c8e40e34`.

Complete-manifest outer parallel scoring remains the later evaluation baseline. Network jitter,
packet loss, latency injection, and network retry stress remain out of scope.
