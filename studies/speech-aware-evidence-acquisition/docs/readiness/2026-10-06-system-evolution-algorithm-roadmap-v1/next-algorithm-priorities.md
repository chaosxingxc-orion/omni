## What learning must change

Retain every original experiment and cumulative exposure. Make a compact, bounded
active state determine subsequent proposals: selected parent systems, abstract
supported/contradicted template hypotheses, and exploration allocation. An archive
that grows while the same parent and generation rule repeat has recorded evidence,
but has not demonstrated an effective policy update. Deletion alone does not solve
this. Require a trace from aggregate feedback to a changed search decision, then
fresh complete-system measurements; retain unsuccessful updates as learning failures.

Our frozen-core, deterministic complete-system template search is training-free
black-box policy optimization. Do not invent policy gradients or describe it as an
exact reproduction of stochastic Training-Free GRPO. Declare each adapted baseline
and any new algorithm under separate prospective identities.

## Priority 1: protect selection from measurement ambiguity

Repeated seed/A/A variation and equal-rendered-request output differences are
established; their runtime root cause remains unestablished. Do not identify a local
optimum from a series of rejected noisy panels. Keep registered A/A, paired intervals,
missingness and unchanged-policy controls. Treat point-estimate search choices as
exploration decisions, not scientific promotion. A repetition or numerical probe
requires a bounded prospective contact registration and does not replay old scores.

## Priority 2: change parent retention before adding optimizer complexity

Compare the frozen current aggregate optimizer with a prospectively registered
bounded multi-parent selector under the same proposal and complete-system evaluation
allocations. Begin with this main algorithm change; keep feedback representation,
model, legal edit scope and fixed data/knowledge comparable. Shared-engineering
corrections apply consistently. Exact counts, node list, modes, bounds and source
identities come from one immutable configuration throughout the lifecycle.

Retain a quality-oriented parent and eligible complementary parents within each
measurement domain. Keep a separately bounded exploration pool; a lower point score
may justify exploration, never automatic deployment. Rotate underused eligible
parents with reproducible selection. Preserve every original candidate, including
no-change and invalid slots; no retries or replacement proposals.

Borrow SkillOpt's evaluated edits and rejected-change memory, GEPA's complementary
candidate selection, and DGM's exploration from historical ancestors. Our aggregate
quality-cost frontier is not GEPA's task-wise frontier. Maintain that distinction.
Do not merge different cohort observations into a global frontier merely because
the input roster or serving identity matches. Common-domain evaluation has a real
budget and must be registered before using it for such selection.

The selector, configured local/recombine/explore planner, and complete-source adapter
already have offline tests. They are not yet a live learning algorithm. Remaining
work includes history-derived usage continuity, proposal reservation integration,
exact algorithm/config identity, actor response contracts and actual comparison.
Do not wire the prototype into the old algorithm name or change Update6 retention.

## Priority 3: make optimizer experience revisable and behaviorally effective

Keep hypotheses about templates and assembly, never task answer memories or knowledge
content. Each experience item must bind its evidence and contradictory aggregate
record IDs, scope and next falsifiable test. Bound active capacity through config.
Retire contradicted, superseded or redundant guidance from active use while retaining
its full immutable history and exposure. Low use is not proof that a rule is false.

Borrow Training-Free GRPO's semantic experience update idea with an explicitly
aggregate-only adaptation. A change to experience must affect an eligible next
proposal or compiled template to count as a behavioral update. A stored ADD/DELETE
operation with no consumer is only metadata. Test this as a subsequent algorithm
factor if selector-only evidence leaves a clear unresolved question.

## Priority 4: explore different behaviors, not paraphrases

Reserve configured slots for local improvements, complementary recombination and
broader distinct mechanisms. Favor changes in evidence use, review decision criteria,
revision restraint or cross-node handoff over cosmetic wording diversity. Preserve
full independent node contexts and final Omni answer authority. Invalid output
contracts remain failures, not automatic prompt-repair opportunities.

Use stagnation to change future registered exploration allocation or eligible parent
families; do not raise decoding temperature, delete adverse results, or silently add
slots. No method guarantees escape from all local optima, and diversification alone
is not evidence of better task performance.

## What the learner and report should see

Keep K aggregate records per observed population and T*K historical records, derived
from configuration. Include complete templates/assembly, official business outcomes,
process statistics, uncertainty, denominators and missingness. Keep detailed sample
contents, raw trajectories, knowledge and source lists in private audit custody.

Business outcomes lead. Reviewer detection must be read alongside false-alarm rates,
repair and damage among eligible revisions. Separate raw model revision from
normalization effects. Coverage/visibility does not establish semantic consumption;
alarming on an erroneous draft does not establish a correct criticism. Process
metrics guide hypotheses without becoming unvalidated causal node rewards.

Report search diversity and concentration, duplicate/no-change/invalid rates, accepted
parent changes, improvement per actual evaluation cost, and unsuccessful exploration.
Latency remains a secondary cost axis. More contacts, more alarms, longer reasoning
or greater lexical diversity are not themselves learning gains.
