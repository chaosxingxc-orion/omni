numeric and insertion analysis. The current-window hypothesis is an uncertain word or entity,
not an SLURP intent label. Preserve the source window and whole-call evaluation population.

  Its state is task evidence; it does not deliver the final transcript.
- Solver drafts the full transcript from the audio and legal supplied context, then retains
