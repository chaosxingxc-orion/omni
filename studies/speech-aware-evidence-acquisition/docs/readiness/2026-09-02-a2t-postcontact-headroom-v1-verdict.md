## Interpretation

The failed pre-contact exact-cell controller was solving the harder problem: predict whether
matched evidence will help before observing its answer. The paired surface shows a simpler and
better-targeted control point after the matched answer already exists. A post-contact controller can
compare M0, matched, retrieved-target support, and registry validity, then choose the final answer.
Its attainable advantage over always matched is bounded above by the six harms it could avoid;
that is enough to justify one model-free screen but not enough to tolerate policy search.

The next screen must therefore freeze one runtime-visible state representation, one nonparametric
reward-memory rule, one fold assignment, and its gates before any conditional reward stratum is
read. It must compare against both M0 and always matched. Threshold, bin, feature, distance, or
candidate search on these 104 outcomes remains forbidden.

The 2026-09-02 owner direction requires a task-wide knowledge ground that the frozen core can
understand and use to reach at least 90% basic task performance, with 95% as the target, before
candidate-set pruning or controller optimization resumes. This audit reinforces that order: even
the descriptive perfect arbiter over the current M0 and matched responses reaches only 46/104

separately preregistered, query-independent, non-gold-conditioned knowledge-ground ladder is
qualified. Failure of the complete ground opens knowledge construction, not policy tuning.
