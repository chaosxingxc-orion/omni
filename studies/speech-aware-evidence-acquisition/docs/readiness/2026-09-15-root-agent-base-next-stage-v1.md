The immediate milestone is achieved within exposed SLURP discovery: the same frozen agent package improves official task quality in two complete paired comparisons. Preserve that package as the working baseline. Transfer it before claiming generality, and improve it through explicit information and decision mechanisms before claiming a training-free RL algorithm.

## Root-owned reasoning and delegated mechanics

Root personally reads complete results, attributes errors, weighs counterevidence, designs mechanisms and makes admission decisions. Delegated agents may report structured status events, extract exact source facts, implement a root-specified change and run bounded verification. They do not choose mechanisms, judge scientific results or make recommendations on root's behalf. Human-facing explanation remains root's responsibility.

## What the evidence says

Candidate coverage is 83.1 percent in the full SLURP replica, below the owner's 95 percent objective. Coverage-conditioned final accuracy is 756/831, about 90.97 percent. Downstream decisions rescue 11 rank-one mistakes while damaging 20 correct rank-one choices. Meanwhile, 477 cases have correct intent but nonexact entities. These are distinct observable opportunities; improving only one cannot close the entire task gap.

Reviewer sees slot-contract evidence in all 2,018 of its requests, yet only 11 episodes receive a Solver revision. Existing prompts and slot cards already require speech grounding, omission of absent slots and preservation of spoken forms. The next change cannot be justified as merely adding those existing rules or granting access that already exists. High review acceptance alone does not prove calibrated correctness. Static contract lookup supplies schema information, not new acoustic evidence.

Released TRAIN attribution gives three substantial annotation-relative loss groups: filler disagreement, unmatched references and unmatched predictions. Reference-empty cases are frequent. Identify whether an error concerns a missing acoustic fact, task-specific interpretation or annotation scope before selecting a tool or knowledge change. Do not call every extra prediction a hallucination or every filler disagreement an ASR error.

## Next SLURP design questions

- Candidate coverage: determine which observable ambiguity merits another discriminative knowledge lookup or a bounded candidate refresh. Return hypotheses and supporting cues, not a falsely confident top one. Evaluate gold-in-candidate-set offline, preserve actual candidate width, and do not use gold membership as a runtime trigger.
- Candidate resolution: make each retrieved value explain a decision boundary and the evidence that distinguishes the competing interpretations. A later Solver may change the initial ranking, but record the evidence supporting that change and quantify both rescues and harms.
- Entity interpretation: explanatory values should specify semantic role, mention boundaries, allowed representation, task-specific omission conditions and confusable alternatives. Memory should retain reusable corrections and validated rules from TRAIN, not raw training examples disguised as memory.
- On-demand evidence: Reviewer identifies a concrete suspected problem and missing evidence; Planner chooses a permitted observation or check; Solver consumes the result and retains answer authority. An ASR result, a localized speech segment or an alignment-based check should answer that uncertainty. Do not schedule Whisper as an unconditional preprocessing pass.
- Measurement: report full native task quality first, then candidate inclusion, covered resolution, correct-intent entity errors, tool-trigger coverage, revision saves/harms and unresolved conflicts. Tool count or review length is not information gain.

This document is root's design direction, not a new generation registration. A concrete successor must name its changed behavior, observable trigger, legal actions, knowledge/memory sources, comparison and stop rule before contact. Do not reopen a twice-failed flight by renaming it.
