## 1. Why this cell exists

The M1 compression analysis measured that the slot-contract cards are about 49 % of the post-constant-prefix row and that
90.6 % of their characters serve slot types no answer used; the knowledge-index design (owner decision 48) renders the
contracts once, in the constant prefix, as an L0+L1 index, and hands each row pointers plus budgeted expansions instead of
the full cards. Owner decision 50 admits such a rendering form only as a one-factor cell on the effect-cost front, read on
the official number at own-null margins, NEUTRAL-or-better; decision 53 places it on the 0-B base (cell B1-notool: no
role holds a tool, so the prefix is constant by construction). Decision 52 fixed the provisional defaults; the derangement twin (index design section 7.2) is registered beside this cell so content and form are separated (A2T ORG-1 read -3.23 pp from form alone).

## 3. Decision rule (fixed here)

NEUTRAL iff the CI95 lower bound of the paired official pooled SLU-F1 delta (arm minus B1-notool) exceeds -1.2350 AND the
CI95 lower bound of the paired intent delta exceeds -1.2212 (paired whole-speaker cluster bootstrap, 10,000 draws, seed
20260915); ADMITTED iff NEUTRAL and the SLU lower bound is above zero; HARMFUL iff a guard fails with a measured loss

counts, dispositions (finite_completion, react_exhaustion, degradation), Solver citations resolved / unresolved, delivery
