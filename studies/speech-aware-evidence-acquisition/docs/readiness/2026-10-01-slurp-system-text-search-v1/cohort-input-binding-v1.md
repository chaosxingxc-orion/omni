## Compilation contract

`rails.system_text_cohort.bind_system_text_cohort` is a thin input-binding step
after the existing joint policy compiler. It accepts an exact registered TRAIN
cohort, verifies every pinned input, and preserves the compiled policy's role
texts, knowledge versions, slot opportunity, intent ranking and voting rules.
The older bootstrap fragment supplies query and packet identities only. Its
historical intent ranker is neither copied nor activated.

Require `--cohort-binding` on `scripts/system_text_search.py compile-candidate`.
Use the matching `cohort-bindings/update-N-v1.json` and exact runtime view for
that update. Missing bindings fail before compilation. The archive records the
final bound configuration hash, so later stage registration cannot substitute
the old R01C configuration. Parent, A/A and retained historical nominees also
need a configuration bound to their current evaluation cohort; use the same
binding function and preserve their policy bundle identities.

The binder verifies ordered roster identity, unique TRAIN IDs, source hashes,
knowledge snapshot/release pins, exact vote rules, zero-contact agreement
provenance, agreement membership, and coverage by the sealed query files.
It updates both the phase-supply agreement source and its declared consumption
component. Shared module verification checks they remain consistent. It keeps
preparation provenance and removes inherited supply/FIT identities.

The four `slurp-system-text-<stage>-seed-input-proof-v1` baseline fragments are
model-free integration fixtures for the real seed, not admitted generation
cells. `slurp-system-text-cohort-supply-proof-v1` permits only checks and supply
preparation. Each future parent, A/A and candidate still needs its own run
identity, prepared supply, real maximum-request FIT, generation/scoring
registration and coordinator admission. Proof artifacts cannot be renamed into
flight artifacts or used as an effectiveness result.
