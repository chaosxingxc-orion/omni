**Finding 3: the completion cap.** Completion cap 8192 tokens (amended from 2048 at the decode-cost sign-off of 2026-10-11, the one re-registration this finding pre-authorised). Slot budget: -c 98304 / -np 4 = 24,576 tokens per slot. The direct-only prompt is about 2,300 tokens per row (the sealed Instruct direct form measured 2,262 to 2,280 prompt tokens per contact; 3,733 on the A2T registry). 2,300 + 8,192 = 10,492 tokens, 42.7 percent of a slot, so the cap is still bound by decode cost, not by the slot. The first cap, 2048, was proposed because a SLURP answer is a short structured object, so a thinking trace that needs more than 2,048 tokens is itself the signal the read must see (a truncated answer is an abstention, counted in the cap-hit table). The four-row decode-cost smoke then measured the real distribution (run SAEA-SLURP-DIRECT-ONLY-FOLD-B-THINKING-SMOKE-4-V1, attempt attempt-slurp-direct-only-fold-b-thinking-smoke-4-generation-v1, 2026-10-11T21:49Z, 4 calls, 9.9395 speech-seconds): completions of 363, 921 and 977 tokens delivered 99, 111 and 60 characters of content, and ONE of the four contacts spent all 2,048 completion tokens (prompt 2,285) and delivered content of length 0 (the whole budget went to reasoning, which the server routes to message.reasoning_content and the transport never reads), so 1 of 4 rows hit the cap; decode speed was 37 tokens per second per slot (58 with one slot busy) and prompt evaluation 0.9 to 1.7 ms per token. That empty content crashed the OpenJiuwen wave (the sealed state refused an empty string). The harness now seals an empty final content of a direct-only contact as the explicit empty marker and leaves a saea-empty-completion-v1 receipt (cap_hit, completion_tokens, finish_reason), so a cap hit is a row-level outcome counted in the cap-hit table, never a flight abort. A cap that truncates a quarter of the rows would make the read a read of the cap, so it is raised to 8192 for the smoke (re-flown whole under rec1) and the Thinking arm. The Thinking arm's GPU-hour ceiling moves 3.0 -> 4.5: a bound, never a target; at the smoke's mean of 1,077 completion tokens per contact and 4 slots x 37 tokens per second the 1,080-row arm is about 2.2 GPU-h, and 4.5 GPU-h admits a mean of about 2,200 tokens per contact (the all-cap-hit worst case, about 16.6 GPU-h, is not what the ceiling covers). The cap lives in the model fragment's decoding_params and the flight attaches to a resident session by fragment name and sha256, so the fragment (q4km-thinking-mmproj-free-slurp-direct-fold-b-np4-ctx24k-max8192-v1) and the serving session (serving-slurp-thinking-direct-fold-b-np4-max8192-v1) both carry the new cap; the flown q4km-thinking-mmproj-free-slurp-direct-fold-b-np4-ctx24k-max2048-v1 and serving-slurp-thinking-direct-fold-b-np4-v1 stay on disk as the record of the first smoke attempt.
