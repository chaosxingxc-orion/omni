## Read rule (preregistered)

`scripts/analysis/slurp_nxt_r3_solver_template_column_read.py`: every arm's first-visit Solver answer is scored once, complete, by the official scorer (`slurp-slu-official-train`, `stage3_rollout_score --mode supply-variants`); each candidate arm and the twin minus the delivering `lock` arm, SLU-F1 (primary) and intent accuracy (guard), paired per row, utterance-cluster bootstrap CI95 (10,000 draws, seed 20261018). The twin is the in-flight A/A: its SLU delta is the offset o and |o| the bar. Words per candidate arm (fixed here and in the reader; the first that applies):

* **NOMINATED**: SLU delta CI95 lower bound > 0 AND intent delta point >= -0.65 pp AND the SLU delta point is not below the twin bar (the SLU delta point is >= |o|, the absolute SLU delta point of the twin aa against lock).
* **EFFICIENT-NO-LOSS**: neither the SLU nor the intent delta shows a resolved loss (the NX-T R1 NO-RESOLVED-LOSS rule: SLU delta CI95 upper bound >= -|o|; intent delta CI95 upper bound >= -0.65 pp, the decision-190 intent floor) AND the Solver seconds per contact of the arm <= 0.85 x the lock arm's.
* **HARM**: SLU delta CI95 upper bound < 0.
* **NEUTRAL**: none of the above.

Secondaries per arm: the Solver forced-close share (overall and split by supplied-item bin, < 40 and >= 40 supplied items), completion tokens (mean / p50 / p90 / p99 / max), seconds per contact, the empty-gold false-fill share (overall and split by forced status), the non-empty exact-set rate, and the per-scenario table (natural mixture and equal scenario). claims_benefit is false; development numbers only (owner decisions 28 and 86).
