reference, while local anchor associations stay unchanged. Keys, vectors, ranking,

speech packets and decoder weights remain the same. The explicit factor is the
additional aggregate content and its necessary provenance, not a retriever change.

or establish that arbitrary verbosity helps. Preserve the closed negative source-span
