## Scope that remains carrier-specific

The runtime foundation is shared. The current system-text policy ancestry validator still accepts SLURP TRAIN identities, the context-learning protocol binds the fixed SLURP400 pool and its exact source gates, and terminal utility uses official SLURP word/character counts. These are deliberate scientific contracts, not a generic cross-carrier learning interface. Do not pass Audio2Tool or ASR rows through the SLURP utility by renaming fields or waive their official scorers. A future carrier-specific optimizer requires its own source/metric contract while reusing the same execution base.

The consumption hook tables explicitly retain unavailable entries and lack admitted current locks for the other carrier profiles. Their all-off paths and rejection behavior passing tests does not prove that every SLURP component can run there. Preserve those refusals until the carrier-specific mechanism and system acceptance are actually established.
