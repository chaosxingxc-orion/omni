# Complete-system semantic comparison runtime

## Purpose and status

Advance the pinned TF-GRPO adaptation from whole-episode summaries to a
model-facing comparison of complete delivered-system outcomes. Bind the
summaries actually generated by the frozen core to the exact actor episodes
and official terminal counts. Do not substitute root-authored summaries,
component scores, normalized group advantages or reference answer text.

This change prepares and connects the comparison stage. No real research
summary, semantic comparison, experience update or reexecution has run.
The sole candidate recovery remains execution-closed. Its isolated control

## Source and terminal-feedback binding

Use analysis.system_summary_source.verify_system_summaries to read a
COMPLETED, FINALIZED, scoreless prepared-context-text artifact. Recompose its
four fragments from the exact retained clean checkout. Verify the bundle,
construction authority and full output population. Rebuild the complete
actor contexts from their registered source. Reconstruct each request's
prepared history and phase instruction and join its response bytes by
request identity and text hash.

Retain empty, capped, unknown-completion and nonstandard-finish summaries
with explicit status. Do not repair their text, retry generation or substitute
a different request. A historical preparation keeps its recorded implementation
identity; the reader rechecks its content against the complete source and
frozen request rather than requiring the current reader's implementation hash.
New preparation and runtime loading still require their current exact input
and implementation fingerprints.

Use rails.system_comparison_context for shared immutable preparation.
Its DATA-local source document has exactly these fields:

- schema: saea-system-comparison-source-v1.
- round_id: the same construction round used for every actor materialization.
- frozen_f0: the preregistered baseline ratio, unchanged by the comparison.
- members: at least two complete sources, each with a unique label, pinned
  summary_manifest, absolute summary_repo_root and pinned feedback_export.
- comparison_roster: a pinned registered ready-group TRAIN roster, or null
  for a diagnostic preparation without runtime authority.

Every pin is an exact relpath/sha256 pair confined to DATA. Each summary must
have the same model fragment and serving-receipt hash as the other summaries.
Rebuild each feedback export through load_system_feedback, including its
full native source, seal and official scoring authority. Require its manifest
and phases to match the summary's actor. The existing rollout grouping
checks full actor roster order, input, model and serving identity; the
semantic join checks speech/split/round, all episode bytes and delivered
answers. Retain official combined word/character TP, FP and FN, intent
correctness and format separately.

The signed construction utility remains 2*TP - F0*(2*TP + FP + FN).
It distinguishes correct empty entities from false positives. It is not a
success probability and need not rank improved systems by pooled SLU-F1.
The final scientific verdict must still use the complete official task
metrics, protections and matched comparison.

## Population and model context

Keep every group in the derived comparison report. Classify it as ready,
no-update-terminal-tie, or blocked-unusable-summary. A ready group has at
least one difference in terminal TP/FP/FN, intent or format, and all its
summaries are usable. Different texts alone do not invent a reward signal.
Keep raw unusable summaries and all terminal ties in the full report;
do not hide them in a success-only analysis.

The execution roster must equal the ordered ready groups exactly, with
SLURP TRAIN construction identities. A diagnostic null roster does not
authorize runtime loading. A future registration must freeze the eligibility
rule and commit the derived roster before model contact; preparation is not
permission to launch.

Present anonymous option labels, recorded summaries and their paired terminal
feedback to the model. Keep run/attempt/request/episode mappings in separate
custody records. The current role prompt asks for cross-role and final-delivery
comparisons, contradictory outcomes, harms, and up to three conditional
lessons in an Experiences block. The three-lesson cap and task-specific count
feedback are explicit adaptations, not identical upstream prompts. Preserve
the raw comparison response; proposed lessons are provisional and do not
directly update memory or acquire final-answer authority.
