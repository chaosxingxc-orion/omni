## Decision

Prioritize testing a short evidence-consuming path without mandatory model self-review.
Keep external official evaluation, process measurement and complete-family qualification.
A small active-parameter count does not itself establish a model's reasoning ceiling,
training deficiency or inability to benefit from specific verification. Treat instruction
overload and ineffective review as hypotheses to test, not settled model diagnoses.
Keep offline policy optimization outside the frozen runtime model: an external proposer
improves templates from aggregate system rewards; Omni executes bounded task operations.
