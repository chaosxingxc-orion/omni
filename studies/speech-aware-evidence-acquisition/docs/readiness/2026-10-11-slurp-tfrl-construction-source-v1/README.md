## What this is

The proposer's tables and the ACE reflector's material come from ONE lock column flown on the CONSTRUCTION roster `SLURP-STAGE3-CONSTRUCTION-R01C` (R01C, 625 TRAIN rows outside Fold A and Fold B), never from Fold B. Fold B is the held-out selection split; Fold A confirms. The reason is the construction-roster vs knowledge guard of 2026-09-23: the semantic-knowledge preparer forbids Fold B rows as carrier ids of a delta-store registration, so ACE playbook releases staged from a Fold B column are refused.

The cell `slurp-native-stage3-tfrl-construction-lock-r01c-v1` (+ `slurp-native-stage3-tfrl-construction-lock-r01c-smoke-4-v1`, four rows) is the review-off COMP-1b module graph with NO columns and NO supply arms: the plain lock, program `phase-native-module-v1-c2`, 2 contacts per row, lock `v2.2` plus the CONSTRUCTION VARIANT `r01c` (requests byte-identical to lock v2.3 apart from the inert ace-playbook store, proof P-ACE-0; v2.2 because the ace i0 anchor cannot be re-homed, see the resolved blockers). Scorer `slurp-slu-official-train` (per-row official scores on disk), `stage3_algorithm` `shared-rollout`, measurement design `material-source` with `claims_benefit` false. It is the material source for iterations 1-3 of the typed loop; no benefit claim; it is re-flown only if the lock changes.

## How the loop uses it

* `open --iter t` takes the tables (and so the ACE reflector materials) from THIS column for t = 1, 2 and 3, because the lock does not change between iterations.
* Iterations 2-3 additionally read the previous iteration's Fold B read, ONLY for (a) the refused-candidate buffer and (b) the ACE counters. Counters are credit (helpful / harmful / scoped_rows) computed by script over the Fold B `ace` column versus `lock`; they are numbers, not knowledge, so they may flow from the evaluation fold. No Fold B row, table cell or text is a knowledge source.
* The ACE playbook releases i1+ are staged with R01C as the construction scope and the committed `stage3-deltas-v1r` registration's permitted and forbidden rosters (permitted: SLURP-STAGE3-ADVANTAGE-R01C-DISAGREEING-V1, a subset of R01C; forbidden: Fold A, Fold B, DEV-1000), so the preparer accepts them.
