## Design within the configured slots

First summarize the authorized evidence internally in three columns: observed BUSINESS/PROCESS/COST pattern; uncertainty or missingness; candidate mechanism worth testing. Focus on consumption of fixed evidence, useful criticism, correct-draft preservation and repair yield. An uncertain aggregate pattern can motivate a test; it cannot establish a root cause. Every historical comparison stays with its own seed/A/A and cohort. Do not pool repeated inputs as independent observations or use absolute cross-cohort rankings as causal guidance.

Before drafting role text, assign every configured slot a primary mechanism from the bound matrix or an equally concrete permitted mechanism, an edit scope, an assembly setting, a predicted aggregate signature and a failure signature. This is an internal design allocation, not an extra emitted population or a request for more slots. Assign no-change only where justified by the active parent's uncertainty or behavior; it remains a spent position, not an independent A/A control. Honor any prospectively reserved no-change slots. Do not reinterpret the current frozen no-change positions as freely replaceable.

Apply the prospectively bound diversity targets from validation-protocol.md to changed slots. Prefer different decision rules over renamed variants. Do not force every role to change. Retain a few focused changes when feasible to make the population interpretable; use coherent joint changes when a handoff or revision mechanism depends on them. Coordinate edited roles so Solver and Planner agree on what a Reviewer finding means. Disagreement in responsibilities is a defect, not diversity.

Use the branch matrix as a design menu, not a common paragraph to paste. Give related variants a contrasting decision condition. Where assembly is varied, explain which visible information or placement is expected to matter and its possible cost; keep tools, supplied facts and legal authority fixed. Full history and current-state review are both legal hypotheses. Do not impose a population-wide switch to one because it sounds cleaner or caches better.

Write complete replacement English text for every edited role. Omitted roles inherit the exact active parent. Preserve each role's executable schema and legal capabilities. Keep substantive reasoning detail where useful, but remove redundant reminders inside a candidate when their repetition creates conflicting or vacuous rules. Never make the runtime emit internal ledgers, evidence tables, candidate scores or alternate answers unless already permitted by its schema. Internal procedures are bounded single-visit reasoning, not new workflow steps.

## Explain the design using existing metadata fields

Keep rationale a concise string containing: primary mechanism and target; changed-role responsibilities and inherited behavior; source record identifiers and a relevant aggregate measurement with denominator/missingness/uncertainty or an explicit unavailable marker; nearest sibling and the abstract decision condition distinguishing it; assembly reason when changed. Do not paste full histories into rationale. Use only capsule record identifiers, never individual task identifiers or source lists.

Use hypotheses as an array of short English strings covering: (1) predicted business/process direction and the existing aggregate metrics that can test it; (2) a tradeoff or adverse signature that would weaken the mechanism explanation; (3) the evidence/visibility condition under which no improvement is expected. State directions, not invented effect sizes or guaranteed gains. If a desired metric is absent, name it unmeasured and use available proxies cautiously; do not demand per-case export. Do not write JSON objects inside hypotheses.
