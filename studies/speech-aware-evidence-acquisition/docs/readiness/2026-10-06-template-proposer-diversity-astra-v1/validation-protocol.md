# Validation and later assessment

Status: prospective proposer-prompt rubric, not a new scientific acceptance gate. The only implemented audit in this package is audit_static.py, which measures historical lexical/assembly structure from hash-pinned allowed files. It is not a semantic judge, runtime checker, proposal generator or substitute for the shared compiler.

## Before contact

Freeze the prompt version, interface summary, matrix, rubric, experiment config, admitted model/effort, complete aggregate input manifest, output contract, attempt reservation and exposure binding. Fix diversity thresholds before emission or results. Ensure N is unique configured inputs, M is configured nodes, K is configured candidate positions, and T is complete authorized historical populations. The actor receives exactly T*K aggregate candidate records, with K in each population and controls referenced separately. Keep the exact record order and all dispositions. Confirm every parent and historical full role text and assembly setting is accessible within admitted context/byte limits without per-input expansion. If context capacity is insufficient, prepare an exactly deduplicated, hash-resolved complete view prospectively; do not truncate unfavorable records or make a new unbounded read opportunity.

Use the current shared aggregate exporter and compiler. The actor's own mechanical pre-emission serialization checks are not permission to query a compiler repeatedly. After first emission, archive the exact bytes and metering, then validate once through the normal pipeline. An invalid/no-change position remains spent. Diversity warnings never trigger replacement, resampling, selective omission or another semantic call.

## Structural review

Require exact top-level/branch/proposal fields; K and order; one correct active parent; algorithm/iteration/input bindings; changes restricted to the supported surface; complete edited role texts; full exact assembly objects; no knowledge edits; no added runtime schema fields or authority; and original byte/per-text caps. Audit the full inherited system rather than only the patch. Check that revision and tool instructions defer to current native limits and that only Solver emits the task answer. Validate English. Compiler parsing alone does not prove operational role/schema compatibility, so retain the registered full-request capacity and structural proofs before any eventual rollout.

## Lightweight semantic rubric

Score each changed candidate on four dimensions, 0 (absent), 1 (vague), 2 (operational and supported). This score describes proposal quality, not task reward or historical acceptance.

| Dimension | A score of 2 requires |
| --- | --- |
| Targeting | A capsule record/aggregate pattern with cohort and uncertainty; a corresponding failure mode; no invented task case or causal attribution. A missing metric is named rather than guessed. |
| Executable mechanism | The edited text specifies evidence order and a decision/repair condition that fits existing schemas, visibility and limits. The metadata promise appears in actual role text. |
| Distinction | A nearest sibling and an abstract condition under which the policies intentionally differ; the differing sentence/setting can be located. Synonyms or an irrelevant bit flip do not satisfy this. |
| Falsifiability and tradeoff | An available business/process metric signature, an adverse signature and a no-improvement condition. Preserving quality remains central; more alarms/calls alone never count as success. |

Report the fraction scoring 2 in each dimension and the fraction satisfying all four, with changed, no-change and invalid denominators separate. The target is all valid edits operationally specified; unresolved deficiencies are logged, not secretly repaired. To check cosmetic diversity, temporarily ignore names, family labels, section headings and generic exhortations; restate each branch's decision rule in one plain sentence. If two restatements match and their visibility/repair rules match, count one mechanism family regardless of lexical distance.

Perform a cross-node swap audit inside each complete candidate: if a Solver paragraph can be pasted into Reviewer or Planner unchanged without violating any responsibility, mark it as generic and inspect whether it actually contributes a distinctive procedure. Shared task facts, legal limits and exact schemas are legitimate repetition; do not penalize their equality. Flag a proposal when its claimed node-specific mechanism is only such shared prose. This review needs no task cases, gold, model calls or long trajectory narratives.

Use the matrix's config-derived family/assembly targets to report unique primary families, maximum family share, focused edits, joint edits, assembly combinations and shortfalls. Count an assembly contrast as assembly diversity even when its prose matches; do not count it as a new semantic family. A family label never overrides the semantic audit. No unique-template quota forces every branch to change every role.

## Lexical diagnostics

Measure exact full-system and per-role hashes, edit/inheritance counts, assembly counts and pairwise same-role word 5-gram Jaccard. Report raw similarity and similarity after removing exact paragraphs shared by at least ceil(0.75*K) candidates for that role. The latter is a sensitivity analysis: common paragraphs include recurring semantics, not just required syntax. High raw similarity can be appropriate; low stripped similarity can still be paraphrase. Never optimize a lexical score alone.

For a prospective pair review, flag high stripped similarity (for example >=0.70) as a review aid only; pin any chosen threshold before emission and do not treat this illustrative value as an established statistical boundary. Also review pairs with identical semantic fingerprints regardless of low word overlap. A fingerprint has: primary target, evidence order, disagreement criterion, repair dependency rule, history assumption and assembly settings. Store fingerprints in the root audit or concise existing rationale; add no fields to the compiler response. Prompts need not have identical scaffolds to be auditable.

## Assess a later registered complete panel

Keep the registered selection/retention rule unchanged. Do not add a diversity bonus to its reward. First verify the whole configured population, controls, official scoring and READ qualification. Export no partial population. Then report BUSINESS (official pooled F1, intent, completion), PROCESS (alarm detection/precision/false alarms, repair and damage including correct-draft preservation, visibility and missingness), and COST (sample time, first/non-first means, contacts and tokens). Keep per-stratum natural-mixture/equal-stratum tables in the root report; they are not individual case exports to the actor.

Decode every process array using the supplied metric_order/rate_fields. Report numerator/denominator/eligible/unknown and interval/missingness bounds; keep model transitions separate from postprocessing. Do not equate alarm precision conditional on draft error with semantic correctness of each criticism. A gain in precision with collapsed detection is not convincing; less harm with no useful repair may simply prevent all changes. Where the schema lacks a semantic criticism-specificity measurement, leave it unmeasured rather than claim it from finding counts or fabricate a metric. No per-case learner access is required.

Compare each candidate with its own in-chain seed and A/A, and retain its parent comparison and cohort/roster identity. Show the distribution over all K, including unchanged/invalid spent positions and missingness, rather than the best branch alone. Distinguish (a) increased designed diversity, (b) different aggregate process signatures, and (c) improved official outcomes. Only (a) is checkable before rollout; even (b) need not imply (c).

N20 reused TRAIN results remain optimization feedback/form proof under the owner amendment, not efficacy or generalization. Small differences inside A/A variation and broad intervals remain inconclusive. Follow the existing larger-panel, same-roster replication, own-null and formal claim rules before asserting a stable benefit. A later population with a different prompt, parents, model or flight does not isolate the causal effect of this prompt. A prompt-effect comparison would need its own prospectively authorized comparable populations and in-chain controls; this package creates none. Preserve TF/GEPA identities and spent history, and make no matched-compute claim where authoritative usage or design comparability is unavailable.
