# Current-A0 Fold A admission and supply

The Fold B registration requires the predecessor's exact roster, 107,264 repeated audio seconds and equal predecessor supply outputs. Those conditions do not describe Fold A. The new registration uses the verified current-A0 Fold A derivation, all 1,080 Fold A identities and its 104,874-second structural allowance. It revalidates accepted serving/proof evidence and computes the card-hour ceiling from that measured binding. Generation, inline FIT and saved final-delivery READ receive independent ledger entries and hash-bound plans.

The writer stages a fresh package, keeps the cell held, archives replaced bytes, and refuses spent identities, changed source/configuration, wrong roster/audio, mismatched serving or absent proof. It does not mint or contact a model. The standard registration checker is used in tests; after registration its only findings are the expected pending readiness, missing decision receipt and unprepared supply binding.

The mint wrapper rechecks accepted proof, exact run/attempt identities, composed-config freshness and every antecedent hash before using the standard atomic mint. It refuses changed antecedents instead of letting rehashing bless them silently. The supply wrapper requires that decision and a clean unspent held cell, then uses standard `repin_package` with `allow_output_change=False`. Since this cell has no previous supply, it creates its own Fold A artifact; it does not compare its output with Fold B. It refreshes registered supply pins, preserves the minted receipt, and leaves source commit/preflight/dispatch explicitly outstanding. Failure retains partial outputs and an error record.

Research validation: registration 10 tests passed in 24.02 seconds after fixing the first run's missing ledger table header (one failure, nine passes). Mint/supply nine tests passed in 31.07 seconds, including real receipt publication/no overwrite, antecedent/config/proof drift, supply ownership, interruption and receipt mutation. Supply tests substitute the expensive repin producer; actual data-driven repin and full preflight remain unverified.

No actual Fold A evaluation registration, decision or supply was created by these tests. The live A0 source remains frozen. The queued comparator must finish and its configuration be reviewed/committed before integrating the later agreement, evaluation and admission tools. Full-system repeated evidence, paired errors/cost, D0/A0 lock, TFRL and complete 13,078-test comparison remain outstanding.

Public dependency-closed verification: 32 tests passed in 54.83 seconds, including deployment-binding checks and atomic mint durability. The public changes include the required proof/manifest helpers and the narrow shared mint/stale-family fixes.
