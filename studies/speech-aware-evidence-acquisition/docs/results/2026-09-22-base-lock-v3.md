## 5a. The one open harness defect at the lock (item E39)

E35 exposed that E13 `finite_completion_policy = deliver-latest-or-abstain` is scoped to the whole episode: a
`length`-truncated contact on a non-answering node (Perception, 75 of 342 windows on the 1,024-token E21 geometry)
ends the episode with no Solver draft and delivers the empty string. The repair (item E39: `finite_completion_scope =
answering-node`, per-role `max_completion_tokens`, a tool-free Reviewer template for E21) is a base repair, enters as
**base lock v3.1** after its four-row dry run on the E21 geometry, and keeps every sealed configuration byte-identical
by default. The SLURP locked plan flew at cap 512 with short Perception outputs; its sealed reads are unaffected and
are not re-flown. See `docs/results/2026-09-22-e21-native-window-flightruntime-e35-read.md`.
