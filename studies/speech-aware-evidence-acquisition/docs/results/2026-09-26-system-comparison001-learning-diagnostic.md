## Algorithm implication

Sampling complete trajectories and attaching a system reward are necessary
but insufficient. The semantic credit step must preserve event identity,
temporal order, actual capability/observation provenance and metric meaning.
Otherwise an upstream narrative error is promoted into evidence and then into
a reusable rule. An improved terminal score cannot certify that causal story.

This observation concerns the current frozen-model TF-GRPO adaptation on one
construction group. It does not establish that every training-free RL method,
every summary, or the upstream implementation fails. The initial actor also
has the documented tool/prompt mismatch; correction of that mismatch is an
engineering change and must be shared by learned and empty-memory controls.
