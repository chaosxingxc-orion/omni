# Current A0 Fold A diagnostic columns

## Scope

`fold_a_current_column_read.py` registers an independent READ over the complete 1,080-row current-A0 Fold A campaign. It binds the actual admitted source commit, completion records, exact Fold A roster, source configurations, saved final-delivery READ and implementation hashes. Registration lives in a separate analysis checkout, preserving the executed source and its ledger.

The six columns are the five registered first-Solver branches plus the L1 sample-zero stream. Each receives one complete standard SLURP official scoring job through `stage3_rollout_score.py`; scoring is not sharded and makes no new model contact. Every derived copy and cached score is checked against the sealed parent, render hash, roster hash and exact sample identities before statistics are read.

## Reading rules

Reuse the established utterance-cluster paired bootstrap and branch replicate-variance widening, with the same fixed seed, 10,000 draws and replicate set. Report pooled/per-row comparisons and scenario tables. These are within-flight mechanism diagnostics. Historical Fold B parity bands do not apply to Fold A. Branch comparisons do not establish complete-system Direct/A0 benefit or authorize TFRL promotion.

Gold-intent/N1 diagnostics, complete-system repeat qualification and the final benefit lock remain separate requirements. Failures preserve registration, scoring and read evidence; the adapter refuses stage replay and does not silently rerun missing scorers.

## Queue integration

The `queued` stage requires the exact queue's successful `fold-a-current-campaign` result. It registers the READ, commits only the recipe and exposure-ledger change, scores all six columns, verifies them and publishes the statistical report. Its source checkout is independently frozen; it cannot modify the already queued preparation, admission controller or running A0.

## Validation

Tests exercise real Git/ledger registration, changed parents and inputs, exact-roster cache verification, missing scores, subprocess failures, target-source import binding and no-replay behavior. Numeric integration uses the real paired-statistics functions with synthetic column scores. Official scorer execution is substituted in adapter tests: these tests do not constitute actual Fold A scoring or experimental improvement.
