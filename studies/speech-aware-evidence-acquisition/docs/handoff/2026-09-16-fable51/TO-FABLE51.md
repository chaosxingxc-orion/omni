## My central judgment

We already have a useful older SLURP agent base, with two positive same-roster comparisons
against direct invocation. The central problem is how the system represents, selects and uses
information across roles. More candidates, more retrieved text or another generic review pass
have not established further full-task gain.

Keep two scientific questions separate and connect them through task quality:
get the correct intent into the candidate set, then resolve the intent and its entities.
The second question has substantial remaining headroom even with a correct intent.
Give roles the evidence and distinctions needed for their decisions; do not mechanically
handoff every prior statement or convert a hypothesis into an observation.

Treat the role-context compiler as a shared source-bound information interface. It currently
normalizes history but does not fully project accumulated knowledge by role/candidate.
Treat this as a concrete remaining design opportunity, not a reason for broad infrastructure
work before closing the active experiment.

## My expectations, not claimed results

- Typed context may improve the quality of review and reduce harmful propagation of hypotheses.
  It cannot recover evidence that no role ever obtained, and it may show little aggregate gain.
- Candidate-preserving augmentation may improve gold inclusion while avoiding losses from
  complete regeneration; it can also increase ambiguity and harm final selection.
- Candidate-specific slot meaning and span/omission conventions may offer more immediate
  whole-task value than merely increasing N, because correct-intent slot errors are numerous.
- On-demand acoustic tools may help selected hearing ambiguities. Fixed Whisper results do
  not justify a blanket ASR stage, and a bad selector can erase tool benefit.
- A useful optimization space should contain observable, exercised decisions with both gains
  and harms. Larger action inventories alone are not progress.
- Actual training-free RL should follow a stable base and implement sampling and updates.
  Root-authored system improvements are engineering, not an algorithmic result.

The detailed design file states what evidence would support or weaken each expectation.
Do not promise a numerical gain or change a decision threshold after seeing the result.
