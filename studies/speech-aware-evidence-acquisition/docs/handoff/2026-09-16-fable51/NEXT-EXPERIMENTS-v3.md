Author: Codex, root scientific coordinator. Date: 2026-09-16.

## 1. Root assessment: the useful base and the missing capability

A replicated exploratory system gain already exists: the older max512 dual-store SLURP
agent improves official pooled SLU by about 14 points over matched direct on NEW500.
Do not restart the project as if no useful base existed.

The remaining question is whether the system can acquire and use task-relevant information
selectively, with traceable choices that later optimization can improve. Candidate coverage,
candidate resolution, slot interpretation and beneficial review are separate failure links.
The tested maximum-five and broad/factored variants did not establish progression. The more
complex typed-context package failed execution before complete quality evidence.

My selected next scientific change remains candidate-bound slot explanations on the useful
older agent. First fix the exact representation mismatch exposed by its engineering proof.
Do not simultaneously add review prompts, new knowledge, ASR, refresh and deduplication.

The rationale is an observed consumption gap: 525/1080 anchor rows already have the correct
final intent but nonexact entities; cards can already be visible while extraction fails.
This is not a promise that all 525 are recoverable, nor proof that hearing is irrelevant.

## 3. First scientific experiment: candidate-bound slot views

**Question.** Can the same facts, organized for the current candidates and latest draft,
improve full SLURP performance over the already useful agent?

**Treatment.** At each Solver/Reviewer request, derive a view of each selected slot card
that retains bindings for the union of valid candidates and the latest valid draft intent.
Preserve a legal draft outside the candidate list. Preserve global extraction, formatting,
omission, ambiguity and limitation rules. Where scope is invalid or no binding applies,
retain the full canonical card. Keep original source bodies and separate derived identities.

**Control.** The unchanged retained dual-store agent, through the same controller, executor,
serving identity and complete roster. Each arm generates its own complete episode. A replay
of a sealed predecessor is not a complete live-system comparison.

**Held fixed.** Core and decoding; prepared query speech; original role instructions and
bounds; K8/max3; legal inventory; both source stores; selected card IDs/order/caps; analytical
tool authority; delivery and scoring. No concurrent reference-dedup policy or new ASR.

**Roster and metrics.** Complete TRAIN A1080, 125 speakers, 18 scenarios. Prospectively freeze
official pooled SLU micro F1 as primary; 10,000 paired whole-speaker bootstrap draws, exact
seed and CI95; natural-mixture and equal-scenario reporting; full per-scenario table.
Retain all malformed, saturated, fallback and unchanged rows.

My recommended progression rule is a positive whole-cohort difference whose CI95 lower
bound exceeds zero versus the fixed agent, plus engineering validity. This is a proposal,
not an already registered gate. Any protection metrics and interpretation of tradeoffs must
be fixed before outcomes. Do not invent vetoes after reading results.

Report intent/scenario/action, full-task and entity exactness, omission/addition/span/type
errors, actual context sizes, exposure and review outcomes separately. Own-correct and
common-correct-intent subsets are descriptive, with denominators shown; neither substitutes
for the primary whole cohort.

**Direct anchor.** The main user question remains system versus direct model. The view
comparison asks a narrower progression question. Reuse only a released compatible direct
anchor whose source/protocol/service differences are explicit; otherwise propose a fresh
matched direct arm and budget. The closed pair's unread score is not reusable by convenience.

**Expectations and interpretation.**

| Observed result | Interpretation and action |
| --- | --- |
| Lower irrelevant context and positive replicated whole-task gain | Adopt the view; preserve evidence of which errors changed. |
| Fewer bytes/tokens but no quality gain | Useful engineering result only; retain the old scientific base. |
| Better slots but worse final intent or overall SLU | The removed context or decision tradeoff matters; do not promote on the favorable subset. |
| No actual changed view, mostly fallback, or wrong representation | Engineering intervention was not exercised as intended; no mechanism-effect claim. |
| Better valid JSON/review formatting without saves | Structural compliance improved, not task capability. |
| Negative or inconclusive full comparison | Close the tested version and retain the old base; no scope/seed sweep to find a pass. |

The source enumeration's 42.5421% lower ideal card-body bytes for candidate triples is not
a forecast of actual wire tokens, capacity, time or quality. View metadata has overhead.
The proof's 20,417-token margin was Perception-only and is not a Solver capacity result.

## 4. Role-context design: choose information for a decision

Use the agent framework's existing role transitions and tools. A shared deterministic
context compiler should normalize, filter and bind information; it must not secretly act
as another answering model or certify the truth of model-generated text.

| Role | Useful information | Distinction to preserve | What to measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Perception | Speech, complete legal intent inventory, compact activation and neighboring-intent contrasts | A candidate is a hypothesis; retrieval is not a legal whitelist | Gold-in-candidates, actual width, gained/lost cases |
| Solver | Speech, all plausible candidates, relevant semantic roles and extraction/representation rules | Examples/defaults are not observed values; candidates are alternatives | Full intent/slot output, additions, omissions, boundaries |
| Reviewer | Current draft, implicated fields, plausible alternatives, source-grounded contracts and evidence | Criticism is a claim; a citation does not prove entailment; same core errors are correlated | Issue validity after release, tool information obtained, revision saves/harms |
| Planner | Concrete unresolved issue, available legal actions, expected evidence contribution, bounds | Route or stop; do not author a new answer | Executed versus merely declared actions, useful continuation, avoidable repetition |

Separate observation, stable knowledge, hypothesis, draft, critique and action result in the
trace. Preserve uncertainty and conflicting observations. Filter stale role opinions rather
than mechanically handing the entire transcript onward. This longer-term table is not a
license to change every role in the slot-view experiment.

Knowledge construction and consumption should share identifiers and explicit relations:
intent activation keys lead to discriminative values; candidate intents lead to the relevant
slot semantic bindings. Construction-observed support is not a mandatory-slot schema.
Keep the legal inventory independent of construction frequency.

## 5. Candidate-coverage line after the first decision

**Hypothesis.** A bounded evidence-driven supplement that preserves original hypotheses
can improve inclusion more reliably than asking for a larger newly generated list.

Anchor inclusion is 937/1080. Reaching 95% requires at least 1026, a net increase of 89.
Maximum-five gained 19 and lost five. Broad/factored gained 30 and lost 20.
These observed gains do not support a promise that a minor width increase will reach 95%.

A proposed mechanism is to preserve the original normalized list; let Reviewer identify a
specific activation conflict; let Planner authorize at most one distinct-evidence acquisition
and candidate refresh; deterministically union original and supplemental legal IDs.
A provisional maximum of three original plus two supplemental candidates is a design choice
to freeze later, not current permission.

The complete legal inventory remains available. Do not select trigger rows with gold,
inject gold into candidates, infer confidence from fewer candidates or constrain Solver
so tightly that legitimate outside-set recoveries become impossible without study.

Compare one enabled route with the unchanged selected base, on the same complete roster.
Report initial/final inclusion, gains/losses, final intent conditional on inclusion,
full-task quality, real evidence novelty, trigger frequency and actual contacts.

Expected signature: within an episode, a correct union never discards an original candidate.
Additional inclusion may still worsen resolution or slots. If that occurs, the coverage
module improved reachability while downstream use remains unresolved; do not call it a
whole-system improvement. Continued repeated misses require a named source/observation
gap, not an automatic next N/K sweep.

## 6. Intent/slot-resolution line and selective speech tools

If the slot view works, or if released diagnostics identify a missing rule, choose ONE
subsequent intervention:

- Revise value content only from permitted construction data: neighboring-label exclusions,
  semantic role under each intent, boundary/representation rules and ambiguity/omission.
  Register this separately from projection. Do not insert evaluated answer fillers.
- Add one on-demand local observation for a named acoustic uncertainty. Reviewer identifies
  the issue; Planner decides continuation; Solver consumes the result and answers.

For an ASR tool, preserve transcript, uncertain spans, timestamps, source identity and
disagreement with original speech interpretation. Calling it on every row is a different
fixed-preprocessing experiment. The existing fixed-Whisper comparison was not a clear
whole-system improvement; it does not settle selective use.

Other possible tools should match the failure:
localized replay/cropping for span ambiguity; alignment to locate a hypothesized phrase
without pretending it independently verifies that phrase; VAD for speech boundaries;
diarization for speaker-dependent multi-turn tasks. Enhancement can distort speech and
is outside the immediate queue. Local model availability is not evidence of integrated value.

Expected benefit is limited to cases with a resolvable missing observation. A semantic
boundary error may need a better contract, not another transcript. Record selected issue,
tool attempt/failure, new evidence, destination visibility, decision and final save/harm.
A call, citation or longer explanation alone is not information gain.

First compare the fixed agent with one added capability. If later claiming that targeting
beats random invocation, register a matched targeting control with equal opportunity/cost
distribution and complete delivered cohorts; the simple system comparison cannot establish it.

Reuse the qualified resident decoder configuration. If a local tool does not fit the
registered memory envelope, plan explicit exclusive windows or qualified CPU execution.
Do not co-load weights, offload the core or change geometry mid-comparison implicitly.

## 7. When the base is ready for actual training-free optimization

The base is ready when it retains matched task benefit, completes reliably, and exposes
actual bounded choices with observable outcomes. It need not make every component helpful.
It does need more than almost-always-ACCEPT plus unused tool definitions.

Keep these three objects distinct:
1. Direct frozen model.
2. Fixed agentic system.
3. The same agent with an automatically updated external policy/experience mechanism.

A fourth unchanged-policy sampling control is needed to separate policy improvement from
simply spending more inference. Researcher-selected prompts and manually chosen winning
rules do not count as the algorithmic update.

### Proposed optimization objects

| Object | Prospective definition |
| --- | --- |
| State | Deployment-visible candidates, draft structure, source-grounded issues, observed tool history, remaining bounds; no gold or future turns |
| Action | A finite registered set, initially one family such as accept/revise/targeted knowledge/acoustic observation; mask unavailable actions |
| Trajectory | Versioned states, legal actions, exact policy/seed, supplied evidence, calls, source hashes, terminal Solver output and failure state |
| Reward | A frozen score-only function from permitted TRAIN outcomes; raw gold/reference text never enters the acting context |
| Policy | An explicit external distribution or governed rule-selection mechanism with logged assignments |
| Update | A specified automatic operator; no root hand-edit of winners inside the reported loop |
| Evaluation | A frozen checkpoint rule and held evaluation boundary; no adaptive selection on evaluation outcomes |

Do not optimize the full Cartesian product of retrieval width, context format, tools,
revision count and stopping at once. Choose one factor family with real executed variation
and a plausible link to outcome. A one-step routing problem is a contextual bandit; call it
that if no sequential credit assignment is studied.

### Sampling and update sketch

Freeze policy theta for an iteration. For each eligible TRAIN state, sample a registered
group of trajectories from that same policy with distinct pinned random streams.
Different historical policies are not a valid same-policy rollout group.

A group-relative signal can take A_i = r_i - mean(r_group), with any variance normalization,
tie handling and clipping fixed beforehand. It is meaningful only if r_i comes from an
executed trajectory and the policy has an actual defined distribution or verbal update
operator. A confidence number in model text is not a sampling probability or advantage.

Possible first baseline: an explicitly adapted training-free GRPO experience/policy update.
Use the existing [2026 implementation audit](../../readiness/2026-09-11-training-free-rl-2026-source-audit.md)
and [source index](../../readiness/2026-09-11-training-free-rl-2026-source-index.json).
These are prior pinned research notes, not a claim of a new literature search in this handoff.
Choose the concrete code/update operator only after the workspace is characterized.

Four rollouts per input and two update rounds are preliminary planning values only. Freeze
the true sampling count, contact budget, reward, state matching, update schedule and
checkpoint selection before contact. Allow alternative inference outcomes deliberately;
temperature-zero repeated calls alone do not define an informative trajectory sampler.

For per-trajectory rewards, declare the relationship between a bounded per-sample proxy and
the official full-cohort pooled SLU objective. Do not equate mean per-row F1 with micro F1.
Keep terminal full-cohort evaluation primary and audit metric mismatch. Failed/rejected
actions stay in the trajectory and accounting.

Useful comparisons are fixed agent, unchanged-policy grouped sampling, and updated agent
under an explicitly matched inference protocol. Missing counterfactual branches have no
measured reward; do not assign them fabricated returns. Explain any extra update-time
computation separately from deployment-time performance.

Expected first result is measurable, reproducible policy variation and evidence whether it
helps. All tied rewards, no exercised alternatives, gains explained by extra sampling, or
failure after freezing evaluation weaken the algorithmic case. Do not promise large early gains.

Fold B remains unavailable for tuning. The legacy full-TRAIN ontology provenance is already
disclosed; do not describe a later evaluation as a pristine independently constructed test
without auditing that boundary. No paper-scale generalization claim is admitted here.
