## 3. Candidate inclusion: preserve and augment hypotheses using distinct evidence

**Question.** Can the system raise correct-intent inclusion without losing useful initial
candidates or degrading final intent/slot quality?

**Evidence motivating it.** N5 improved inclusion by only 14 net rows and lost five previously
covered cases. Broad/factored gained 30 but lost 20. One hundred rows missed the correct intent
in all three released systems. Gold-card availability can be high while generated inclusion
remains low. Increasing N is therefore not the whole mechanism.

**Proposed mechanism.**

1. Preserve the original normalized candidate set and its source. Do not regenerate it in
   place or overwrite its hypotheses.
2. Let Reviewer identify a specific conflict or unresolved activation distinction using only
   speech, candidates, available knowledge and observed tool results.
3. Let Planner decide whether a bounded acquisition/refresh is justified. Retrieve targeted
   neighboring-intent distinctions or another registered observation addressing that gap.
4. Produce a separate supplemental candidate result through the same frozen core.
5. Form a deterministic, source-bound union. Keep original candidates first; deduplicate
   legal IDs; add only as many supplemental candidates as the registered cap permits.
   A provisional design is at most three original plus two supplemental candidates,
   with one refresh. Freeze the exact cap before contact.
6. Let Solver resolve one intent and its entities using the union and associated contracts.
   Preserve disagreements and distinguish refreshed hypotheses from independent observations.

The task's complete legal inventory remains available. Knowledge retrieval must not silently
become a whitelist. Do not add gold to a candidate set, select trigger rows using gold,
infer confidence from a short list, or hard-delete a legal alternative to improve a metric.

**Control and admission.** Compare the unchanged selected agent base with this one added route
on the same complete roster and serving identity. Retain the direct-model anchor for overall
system benefit; reuse it only where protocol/source compatibility is established. This is a
new system extension, not a retrospective replay of the failed dynamic-tools registration.
A shared four-row dry run may prove engineering after admission; it cannot establish gain.

**Primary and diagnostics.** Keep whole-task quality as the progression outcome. Report initial
and final candidate inclusion, gains/losses, actual widths, common-covered final intent,
final choices outside the union, complete slots, trigger frequency and real evidence obtained.
The owner target is at least 1,026/1,080 gold-covered rows (95%), not a promised first-run result.

**Expected signature.** Initial coverage stays identical within each episode; a correctly
implemented union cannot discard an original candidate. Coverage can increase after actual
refresh. Whether those additional hypotheses improve final answers remains empirical.

**Contrary outcomes and stop.** Higher inclusion with worse choice or slots means downstream
ambiguity/consumption is unresolved. Frequent refresh with repeated evidence is not success.
If the candidate-preservation invariant fails, treat it as an engineering defect; if the
full-task gate fails, close the tested version instead of sweeping caps until one passes.
A second execution failure still obeys the inherited recovery limit.

**Unresolved before registration.** Exact trigger schema, permitted new information source,
bounded action mask, candidate cap, actual capacity and all contact budgets. No numerical
model-confidence threshold is assumed calibrated.

## 4. Intent resolution and slots: detailed rationale and conditional knowledge revision

**Question.** Can supplying the relevant semantic role and extraction boundary to the right
node improve resolution and entities without adding unsupported values?

**Evidence motivating it.** The anchor has 525 correct-intent/nonexact-entity cases. Date
knowledge was already visible in all 84 audited omission cases. Current slot cards include
all of a slot type's intent bindings, and all accumulated knowledge reaches later roles.
Some legacy examples entered a harmful revision. Availability is not successful consumption.

**Selected first mechanism: projection, not simultaneous reauthoring.**

Section 2 and the selected candidate-bound design are the executable scope. The role-specific
information targets below describe the longer-term architecture, not additional simultaneous
changes to Perception, Planner or the current role prompts.

- Keep both released stores unchanged.
- Extend the shared role-context/supply interface to select the current candidate's relevant
  intent/slot bindings and preserve applicable global rules, uncertainty and limitations.
- For Perception, prioritize activation conditions and named neighboring-intent distinctions.
  Preserve the full legal inventory.
- For Solver, supply the candidate-specific semantic role, extraction cues, supported surface
  forms and omission/ambiguity behavior. Keep evidence separate from illustrative examples.
- For Reviewer, supply the exact current answer, implicated fields, relevant candidate
  alternatives/contracts and source references. Do not flood it with every prior opinion.
- For Planner, provide the concrete unresolved issue and what each legal next action can add.
  It routes rather than answers.

A deterministic projection must not silently reinterpret a rule, discard an applicable
exception, pretend an absent observed binding is illegal, or turn a global card into a
mandatory-slot schema. Preserve the full source and log selected/excluded sections.

**Second, conditional mechanism: knowledge content.** Only if projection does not address an
identified missing rule, construct a new compact value from permitted construction TRAIN.
Possible content includes neighboring-slot distinctions, span inclusion/exclusion, semantic
role under a particular intent, and task-valid representation/omission examples.
Do not inject evaluation fillers or derive a runtime rule from gold-empty membership.
Register content change separately from projection rather than claiming to isolate both.

**Control.** Use the unchanged chosen agent package versus one projected-context version.
Hold role count, tool permissions, source store, decoding and roster fixed for the first
projection experiment. This is system-version progression; do not reopen a large
knowledge-present/absent factorial grid.

**Primary and diagnostics.** Whole-cohort official pooled SLU is primary. Also report common-
correct-intent slot quality, each own-correct denominator, false additions, omissions, surface
distance, type confusions, full-task exactness, malformed answers and actual context size.
For review, preserve supported-issue declarations, insufficient evidence, actual action,
new information, revision and delivered outcome as separate stages.

**Expected signature.** Less irrelevant cross-intent material should make slot interpretation
and review more precise; false additions and boundary mistakes may decline. Removing useful
context could instead increase omissions. No fixed gain is promised. Gains confined to an
outcome-conditioned subset do not establish whole-system benefit.

**Contrary outcomes and stop.** Lower context volume alone is not the objective. More valid
review JSON or more revisions with no task improvement is not advancement. If necessary
content is already present but still ignored, another identical wording instruction is not
a new mechanism. Name the use/decision failure before proposing another variant.

**My expectation.** This has a plausible path to useful improvement because the remaining
slot error mass is large, but the shared frozen model and annotation conventions can limit
it. Treat projection benefit as untested, and preserve the older base if it does not help.

## 5. On-demand speech tools: resolve a named evidence gap

**Question.** Does agent-selected supplementary speech evidence improve actual delivered
answers on cases the agent can identify without labels?

**Trigger meaning.** The owner clarified "high confidence" as confidence that a problem or
missing evidence exists, not confidence the answer is correct. A model's reported confidence
is not automatically calibrated. Require a structured issue and an expected discriminating
observation; predefine any scalar threshold and its calibration data before evaluation.

**Initial scope.** Admit one locally pinned ASR tool for a named acoustic ambiguity.
Reviewer requests investigation; Planner controls continuation/bounds through the shared graph;
Solver consumes the returned evidence and remains answer authority.
ASR transcript, uncertain spans and source timestamps are tool evidence, not final answers.
Preserve disagreement with the original audio/model interpretation.

Do not run ASR on every sample as fixed preprocessing. Do not give Perception or Solver
independent analytical-tool authority merely to simplify wiring. Do not call tools through a
second private experiment framework. Downloaded/local weights do not themselves prove an
integrated or qualified tool.

**Later tool alternatives, chosen only by evidence.** Localized replay/cropping can address
a span ambiguity; alignment can locate a claimed word but is not independent confirmation
of that word; speech-activity tools can delimit speech but do not determine task meaning.
Diarization matters for speaker-dependent multi-turn tasks, not by default for single-command
SLURP. Enhancement can distort cues and would need a separate hypothesis; it remains outside
the present queue. Do not activate every tool as a menu for its own sake.

**Comparison.** Compare the unchanged agent with one on-demand acquisition capability and
the same bounded continuation policy. If a later claim concerns target-selection superiority,
register a matched random/sham-availability comparison with the same per-group invocation
opportunities and full delivered cohort. Do not infer that causal targeting effect from a
simple with-tool system comparison.

**Expected signature.** Some previously unresolved acoustic entities or intents may become
resolvable and revise correctly. Tool output may also conflict, mislead or be ignored.
The closed fixed-Whisper result is a reason to measure selective use, not evidence the
selective version will win.

**Report.** Invocation eligibility, selection, failures, actual observation contents by
source identity, destination visibility, changes in candidate/answer, saves/harms and cost.
A call and a citation are not measured information gain. Count all attempted and unused work.

**GPU rhythm.** Keep one qualified decoder flight at a time. Qualify whether the local tool can run
within the registered resident memory envelope. If not, plan explicit exclusive service
windows; do not silently offload the core, co-load a large model or change the comparison
geometry mid-flight. CPU execution is acceptable only when it preserves the pinned tool
behavior and the shared resource limits. Register the real requests and tool contacts.

## 6. When the base is ready for algorithmic optimization

A stable useful base needs matched task benefit and a traceable workspace with actually
exercised alternatives, not every conceivable tool. Both helpful and harmful transitions can
make that workspace informative. Do not demand that every module individually improve.

Use three distinct objects: direct frozen model, fixed agent, algorithmically optimized agent.
Retain an unchanged-policy sampling control to separate more inference from policy improvement.

The later baseline is an explicitly adapted training-free GRPO:
freeze one policy, sample actual grouped trajectories with distinct registered random streams,
derive score-only rewards from complete official results, update external experience/policy
through a specified automatic operator, and repeat a fixed schedule.
Four rollouts and two update rounds are preliminary design values, not current authorization.

Potential factors, to select after the base audit, are the legal evidence action, source/value
choice, acquisition timing, candidate refresh, revision and stop. Do not begin with the full
Cartesian product. Choose one factor family with observed variation and a plausible link to
downstream quality. Define state using deployment-visible information only.

My preferred research hypothesis is that an external controller can learn when acquiring
particular evidence improves final task quality, including when it harms or is unused.
Estimate value from executed trajectories/continuations; missing branches have no measured
reward. A numerical action update needs a real finite action distribution and logged assignment
probabilities. Verbal confidence and token explanations are not action advantages.

Expected outcome is evidence about learnable decision variation, not necessarily a large first
gain. All-tied trajectories, no exercised alternative, gains explained entirely by extra samples,
or failure on frozen evaluation weaken the algorithmic claim. Do not have root hand-edit
winning rules or select checkpoints inside the reported optimization loop.

Keep TRAIN A optimization separate from untouched Fold B evaluation. Register the reward,
sampling distribution, state matching, update, stopping and checkpoint rule prospectively.
Do not tune to B or treat different-policy historical runs as same-policy rollouts.
Use the existing pinned 2026 implementation audit when choosing the actual update method.
