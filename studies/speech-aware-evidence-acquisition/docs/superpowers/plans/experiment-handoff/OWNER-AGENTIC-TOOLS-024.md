A repeated identical requested operation may return a verified cache hit; record the request, hit, original producer and actual new neural contacts separately. Do not precompute all transcripts and relabel selective cache consumption as a demonstrated on-demand decoder.

These readiness operations do not decide which examples need semantic tools. Keep original speech available and keep tool hypotheses fallible.
